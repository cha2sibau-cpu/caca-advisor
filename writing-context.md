Purpose & context
Arza Nursatya is an advertising photographer based in Jakarta, operating under the studio name HIRA Imaji. The primary use of Claude within this project is producing portfolio and website copy — specifically campaign write-ups and project case studies written in Arza's voice. Success looks like polished, publication-ready text that reflects a thoughtful, process-oriented professional perspective suited to a creative portfolio audience.
Arza's work spans commercial campaigns for major brands (telecommunications, consumer electronics, pharmaceuticals, insurance).

Key recurring production elements include AI-generated environments, on-set lighting engineered to match pre-designed backgrounds, and composite photography that resolves logistical or creative constraints without on-location execution.

Key learnings & principles

Arza values the distinctiveness of Southeast Asian advertising visual language and approaches campaigns through tight conceptual constraints
AI-generated backgrounds are embraced as a genuine creative tool, not a workaround — with honest acknowledgment that quality risk requires iterative effort
Difficulty and challenge are acknowledged in the writing but never dramatized; the tone balances honest craft commentary with quiet confidence


Approach & patterns
Arza provides concise contextual briefs and is comfortable leaving creative interpretation to Claude within that framework. No extensive revision cycles have been needed.

HIRA Imaji website writing — standing format:
Deliver two outputs: a Synopsis (one sharp sentence capturing the project) and a Full Story (first-person narrative with a journalistic sensibility drawing from outlets like NYT, BBC, Jakarta Post, and Vice; wit is welcome when appropriate)
No explicit numbers unless requested
No awkward mid-text self-introductions
Keywords blended naturally into the prose
Minimal use of em dashes

Every HIRA Imaji website piece closes with this exact two-line sign-off:
